About

Who We Are

Al Barakah Media is a halal-focused influencer marketing agency that partners with food and F&B brands across Saudi Arabia to build long-term, barakah-driven growth — without compromising Islamic values.

We help halal-conscious food brands work with influencers the right way: no fake numbers, no haram content, no shortcuts.

From cafés and cloud kitchens to packaged food brands, we design campaigns that feel authentic, halal, and aligned with your audience’s values. Our approach is strategy-first, focusing on quality content, honest reporting, and long-term partnerships.
